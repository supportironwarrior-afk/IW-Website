WHY IRON WARRIORS?

INDIA HAS THE ATHLETES,
NOW IT NEEDS THE STAGE.

Across India, thousands of athletes train every day. They squat, bench press and deadlift with discipline, sacrifice and determination. But talent alone is not enough. Talent needs visibility. Hard work needs recognition. And athletes need a platform worthy of their strength.

IF CRICKET CAN HAVE A GLOBAL LEAGUE LIKE IPL, WHY CAN'T POWERLIFTING HAVE ITS OWN GREAT PLATFORM?
THE INSPIRATION

FROM A GAME TO A GLOBAL MOVEMENT.

The IPL transformed cricket into more than a tournament. It created a powerful league ecosystem where athletes gained visibility, opportunity, recognition and a larger stage.

Iron Warriors draws inspiration from that idea. We believe powerlifting also deserves a professional platform that can bring athletes together and help India become recognised as a nation of powerful competitors.
